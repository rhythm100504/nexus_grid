# Simulation Engine

Simulate 15-minute intervals and allow 15-minute, 30-minute, 1-hour, 4-hour and 24-hour horizons.
Model solar/wind variation, demand, prices, battery SOC, degradation, weather, failures and transmission constraints.

**Scenarios:**
- Solar generation drops 40%
- Wind generation increases 30%
- Electricity price doubles
- Demand increases 25%
- Battery B1 becomes unavailable
- Transmission line reaches capacity
- Storm warning appears
- Weather forecast changes
- Industrial demand unexpectedly increases
- Multiple events happen simultaneously
